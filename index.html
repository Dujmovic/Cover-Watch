    Alerts

    When a bet changes status, like going from covering to not covering, or a team scores in a game you bet on, Cover Watch shows an alert at the top of the screen with a sound. iPhone pauses web apps when the screen locks or you switch to another app, so anything that changed in the meantime shows up the moment you come back. Sound starts after your first tap in the app.

      Lock-screen alerts

      Sends the same alerts to your lock screen, even when the app is closed. It needs the free Cover Watch helper on Cloudflare. If someone else runs yours, ask them for the helper address and your own access code.
